# Polls: usage

A poll answers one question for the group. Options carry a place photo when they come from the Saved list.

<Canvas of={P.LiveAnimatedFill} />

## States

| State | Visual | When |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Gray fill to share | Any open option |
| Your vote | Iris stroke and iris fill, filled check | The option you picked |
| Leading | Iris fill and a Leading tag | Strictly most votes, poll open |
| Winner | Spectrum border and fill, one sheen | Poll closed. The only spectrum element on the screen |

<div className="doc-dodont">
  <div className="doc-do"><b>Do</b>Let people change their vote until the poll closes. Show Change vote, never a confirm dialog.</div>
  <div className="doc-dont"><b>Don't</b>Mark a leader on a tie. Two options at 2 votes are both default.</div>
  <div className="doc-do"><b>Do</b>Show who voted unless the poll is anonymous.</div>
  <div className="doc-dont"><b>Don't</b>Show the winner treatment before the poll closes, even at 6 of 7 votes.</div>
