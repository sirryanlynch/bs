# TripUp · Prism

Prism is the design system for TripUp, a group trip app for iPhone. Groups decide with polls, keep one shared plan, and split costs down to the bottle of wine.

The direction is calm and native: pearl white on a soft prismatic grey, Liquid Glass for chrome only, and a single accent, **Iris #6E5BFF**. The celebration spectrum is held back for three moments: a poll is won, the group is all squared up, a trip milestone lands.

## How this Storybook is organised

- **Foundations** cover colour roles in both modes, the type scale, spacing, radius, glass layers, elevation, motion, icons and photography. Every swatch and sample reads straight from `tokens.json`.
- **Components** list each building block with controls for every variant and state. Stories render inside an iPhone 17 frame at 402 by 874 pt.
- **Patterns** compose full screens from one scenario: seven friends in Lisbon, a dinner poll, a bottle of Douro that Ren and Nic skipped, and the settle-up.

## Toolbar

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| Theme | Pearl (light) or Graphite Pearl (dark). Sets `data-theme` on the root. |
| Motion | Rich motion or Reduce Motion. Mirrors `prefers-reduced-motion`. |
| Glass | Liquid Glass or Reduce Transparency. Mirrors `prefers-reduced-transparency`. |

## Tokens

The single source is `design-system/build_tokens.py`, which writes `tokens.json` and `tokens.css`. This Storybook copies both on every run (`npm run sync-tokens`), so a change rebuilt there shows up here on the next build. Check `design-system/CHANGELOG.md` for what moved.
