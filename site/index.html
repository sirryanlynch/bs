Let’s do
Lisbon.

Seven friends, one dinner, one bill. TripUp takes the group from “where do we eat?” to everyone squared up.

  1. Ari adds Ren to the Lisbon Crew trip.
  2. Ari polls three restaurants. Votes close at 7:30 PM.
  3. The winner goes into the itinerary, booked for 8:30 PM.
  4. Ari logs dinner. Ren and Nic are off the wine.
  5. Debts simplify from six IOUs to three payments.
  6. Everyone settles and confirms.

        03 · Research · Jobs across the trip

        Full JTBD analysis

        The same crew has jobs before, during and after the trip. The prototype shows one slice: the last evening in Lisbon.

        BeforeDuringAfter

        01Before the trip

        Plans, money and logistics before anyone packs.

        Agree on where and whenRoadmap
        Functional
        Lock dates and a place the whole crew can make.
        Emotional
        Feel heard without having to argue for it.
        Social
        Keep the plan from belonging to whoever posts most.
        Tension
        A 40-message thread where the loudest option wins by default.
        Set a shared budgetRoadmap
        Functional
        Agree on a rough spend per person before anyone books.
        Emotional
        Say what I can afford without feeling judged.
        Social
        Keep friends with different budgets on the same trip.
        Tension
        Nobody wants to be the one who names a number.
        Pool the logisticsRoadmap
        Functional
        Keep stays, flights and documents in one place.
        Emotional
        Trust that nothing important is buried in a chat.
        Social
        Share the admin so one person isn't the travel agent.
        Tension
        Confirmations scattered across inboxes and screenshots.

        02During

        Decisions and spending, live, with the whole crew.

        Decide fast as a groupIn the prototype
        Functional
        Pick one dinner spot before the good tables go.
        Emotional
        Make the call without being the bossy one.
        Social
        Everyone sees their vote count.
        Tension
        The thread stalls, nobody commits, and the evening drifts.
        Keep everyone in the loopIn the prototype
        Functional
        Bring a late joiner in from a web link, with no install.
        Emotional
        Ren feels included from the first tap, even on the last night.
        Social
        Nobody has to ask what the plan is.
        Tension
        A late joiner faces days of chat to scroll back through.
        Log spend and split it fairlyIn the prototype
        Functional
        Record the bill once, with line items, as it happens.
        Emotional
        Log it in seconds and get back to the table.
        Social
        Ren and Nic skipped the wine, so they don't pay for it.
        Tension
        Even splits feel unfair, and asking for an exception feels petty.

        03After

        Closing out the money, then keeping the trip alive.

        Settle up without awkwardnessIn the prototype
        Functional
        Turn six IOUs into the fewest payments.
        Emotional
        Close the trip with nothing hanging over anyone.
        Social
        Nobody has to chase a friend for money.
        Tension
        Reminders feel like nagging, so debts linger for weeks.
        Relive and share the tripRoadmap
        Functional
        Collect the places, photos and moments in one recap.
        Emotional
        Hold on to the good part once the money is done.
        Social
        Share something the whole crew is glad to look back on.
        Tension
        The trip ends on a balance sheet instead of a memory.
