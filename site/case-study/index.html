01 · Brief

Trips run on a group chat and a running tab.

Decisions get buried. Money gets awkward. The brief: one scenario, end to end.

The scenario

  1. Ren joins the Lisbon trip on its last night.
  2. Ari starts a dinner poll with three places.
  3. Everyone votes from the push. Taberna wins.
  4. Ari pays $248.70. Ren and Nic skip the wine.
  5. Six IOUs simplify to three payments. Square.

02 · Research

What the market solved, and what it left open.

Eight apps, the iOS 26 guidelines, and published research on group voting.

ScopeDesk research only. I ran no interviews or usability tests. The validation plan is under Metrics.
Splitwise

Simplify debts works, but stays hidden

It is off by default because it "can cause confusion when people aren't expecting it." So TripUp shows the untangling every time, with why each payment exists.

Splitwise help center
WhatsApp polls

The baseline to beat

Fast, but no deadline, no context, and the result sinks under the next 40 messages.

Wanderlog

Great plan, no vote

The last edit wins, with no record of how the group decided.

Troupe

Voting-first is unclaimed

JetBlue's suggest-vote-plan app has been delisted.

Partiful

Guests act from a link

No download to RSVP. The right path for Ren.

Bandwagon effect

Visible tallies move votes

Showing 80% vs 20% support raised individual support by about 8 points. So counts stay hidden until you vote.

Rothschild & Malhotra, 2014

DecisionCallWhy

Guest link and QRFollowedRen votes and pays on the web, no account.
Deadline and tie ruleImprovedCloses at 7:30 PM or when all vote. On a tie, the starter picks.
Leader shown, picks privateDivergedNames next to options add pressure, so picks stay private.

06 · Design system

Prism: one accent, quiet glass.

Tokens, then Storybook components, then screens. The prototype runs the same components.

  • One accentIris #6E5BFF. Owe and owed always pair color with a word.
  • Spectrum for milestones onlyPoll win, Explore done, all square.
  • One icon setPhosphor, regular at 20 and 24, filled for the active tab.
  • Light, dark, Reduce MotionAll from the same semantic tokens.

07 · From finding to screen

Each screen answers a finding.

The two moments the brief cares about most, traced from what the research showed to the call I made and the screen in the prototype.

Finding
Chat polls have no deadline and no context, and the result sinks under the next 40 messages.
Decision
Real places with photos, a live leader, a close time of 7:30 PM, and picks that stay private.
Screen
The live poll, with the leader, who has voted and the close time.
Finding
Splitwise keeps simplify off by default because it can confuse people, so groups face every IOU.
Decision
Read the balance as a sentence and animate six IOUs into three payments, with every net unchanged.
Screen
Balances, where the crew sees who pays whom and why.
